Case Study · Data Visualization

Insight Board

A data-visualization dashboard that turns a decade of research results (2016–2025) into clear, explorable insight — without a data team on standby.

Role Design & Build
Timeline 3 months
Stack React, Recharts, Node
Year 2025
View on GitHub

The Problem

A research team sat on ten years of valuable results locked in static PDFs and spreadsheets. Answering a simple question like "how did this metric trend since 2018?" meant manually rebuilding charts — so most of the data went unused.

They needed a way for non-technical stakeholders to explore the data themselves, without waiting on an analyst for every question.

The Approach

Research

I sat with analysts and stakeholders to understand the questions they actually asked. Nearly all fell into a handful of patterns: trends over time, comparisons across groups, and distributions.

Design principles

  • Make the common questions one click away, not a query away.
  • Let the chart itself be the filter — explore by interacting.
  • Keep the visual language calm so the data stays the hero.

Building it

I built a React dashboard on top of a Node API that pre-aggregates the decade of results. Interactive Recharts views let users filter, drill in, and export — turning static reports into a living exploratory tool.

The Outcome

Insight Board put a decade of research into people's hands. Stakeholders now self-serve answers that used to take days, and the analysts were freed up for deeper work.
