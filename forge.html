Forge

Getting a new engineer from day one to shipping without the panic. An onboarding platform that turns a chaotic first week into a guided, confident ramp-up.

Role Design & Build
Timeline 4 months
Stack NestJS, Laravel, Bootstrap
Year 2026
View on GitHub

The Problem

New engineers lose weeks to fragmented onboarding — scattered docs, unclear next steps, and a constant fear of "bothering" the team with questions. Managers can't see where people are stuck, and hires ramp up at wildly different speeds.

Teams had the knowledge, but it was buried across wikis, chat threads, and people's heads. There was no single path from "first login" to "first commit shipped."

The Approach

Research

I shadowed three new hires through their first two weeks and interviewed their managers. The recurring theme: anxiety came from not knowing what "done" looked like at each stage.

Design principles

  • Always show the next concrete step — never a wall of tasks.
  • Make progress visible to both the hire and the manager.
  • Bake in human checkpoints so it never feels like a solo gauntlet.

Building it

I built Forge with a NestJS API and a Laravel application — resumable onboarding tracks, automated environment setup, and a Bootstrap manager dashboard that flags stalled hires before they fall behind.

The Outcome

Forge gave new hires a clear, confidence-building path and gave managers early visibility into where people needed help. Ramp-up became predictable instead of stressful.
