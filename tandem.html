Case Study · Fintech

Tandem

From "who owes who" to money that finally feels shared. A split-bill and shared-expense app that takes the awkwardness out of splitting costs with friends.

Role Design & Build
Timeline 3 months
Stack React, Node, Tailwind
Year 2026
View on GitHub

The Problem

Splitting expenses with friends is socially awkward and messy. Between group dinners, shared rent, and trips, people rely on mental notes, scattered messages, and clunky spreadsheets — leading to forgotten debts and uncomfortable reminders.

Existing apps treated splitting like accounting software: cold, number-heavy, and built around "settling up" rather than the friendly, informal way people actually track who owes what.

The Approach

Research

I interviewed 12 people who regularly share expenses and mapped how they tracked money informally. The insight: people don't want a ledger, they want reassurance that "it all evens out eventually" without anyone having to nag.

Design principles

  • Lead with people, not numbers — balances framed as relationships.
  • Make settling feel effortless, not like a transaction.
  • Keep the emotional tone light — no red "you owe" alarms.

Building it

I designed and shipped the product end-to-end — a React front end with a Node API, real-time balance calculations, and a friendly notification system that nudges gently instead of demanding payment.

The Outcome

Tandem turned a socially awkward chore into something that feels effortless. Balances stay visible and low-pressure, and settling up takes one tap.
